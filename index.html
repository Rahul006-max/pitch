03 / RISK ASSESSMENT & CLINICAL MITIGATION

Built For Mission-Critical Reliability.

Identified Risk Clinical Impact Technical Mitigation Protocol
AI Hallucination in Triage Under-triaging a cardiac or stroke victim. Fail-Safe Default: Ambiguous caller responses automatically escalate to Code Red P1. Raw audio and transcripts are displayed side-by-side to human clinical supervisors.
Highway Cellular Blind Spot Inability to stream Gemini Live audio on remote highway stretches. On-Device Gemma Model: Runs locally on driver tablet for offline triage guidance and GPS dead-reckoning until 4G/5G handoff.
Private Fleet Operator Trust Reluctance of private ambulance owners to share dispatch availability. Guaranteed Payout & ONDC Architecture: Flat ₹99 network booking fee; 95% of fare passes straight to vehicle owner with instant UPI settlement.
Cold-Start Driver Adoption Drivers struggle with complex software while navigating traffic. Voice-First HUD: Zero typing required; high-contrast night mode with Google Maps turn-by-turn preemption.
