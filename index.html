Follow-up Calls

03 / PROPOSED SOLUTION

AI that turns any voice into instant rescue.

Turns emergency calls in any Indian language into located, triaged cases, and dispatches the nearest ambulance over an open network.

01

Omni-Channel AI Intake

Converses via Phone, WhatsApp, or App SOS in Kannada, Hindi, or English. Asks structured triage questions instantly.

02

Pinpoint Landmark Location

Combines live GPS with spoken landmarks (e.g. "behind SBI near Silk Board") verified against signal strength.

03

Open Dispatch Match

Neutral network matching the nearest suitable ambulance (private, hospital, 108/112) based on reliable live ETA.

CLINICAL GUARDRAILS
